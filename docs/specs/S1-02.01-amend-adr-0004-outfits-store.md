## User story

```gherkin
Feature: Amend ADR-0004 for an on-device outfits store
  As a considered dresser
  I want my saved outfits kept on my device, next to my wardrobe photos
  So that the build team has an accepted storage decision before outfit code depends on it
```

Context: [S1-02](S1-02-manual-outfit-creation.md) (manual outfits from wardrobe photos, device-only). Scope is frontend-only unless the brief says otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Withdrawing consent deletes every saved outfit
  Given I have confirmed that I am 18 or over
  And I have agreed to store photos of my clothes on this device
  And I have saved an outfit named "Weekend" with 2 wardrobe items
  When I withdraw consent and delete my photos
  And I confirm the deletion
  Then my wardrobe is empty
  And my outfits list is empty
```
