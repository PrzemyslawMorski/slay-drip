## User story

Requirement served: F1.1 (18+ gate at sign-up), UX side only. The control is S1-04.01.

```gherkin
Feature: 18+ self-declaration after sign-up
  As a considered dresser
  I want to confirm that I am 18 or over once I have an account
  So that I can reach my wardrobe
```

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: A new account is asked to declare 18 or over before the wardrobe
  Given I have just registered and signed in
  When the app loads my account
  Then I am asked to confirm that I am 18 or over
  And I cannot reach the wardrobe until I answer

Scenario: Confirming 18 or over reaches the wardrobe
  Given I am asked to confirm my age
  When I confirm that I am 18 or over
  Then the app sends the declaration to the API
  And I see my wardrobe

Scenario: Declaring under 18 ends the session and shows the adults-only message
  Given I am asked to confirm my age
  When I say that I am under 18
  Then the app sends the declaration to the API
  And I see a message that Slay Drip is only for adults
  And my session is cleared

Scenario: A failed declaration can be retried and is not reported as done
  Given I confirm that I am 18 or over
  And the API answers that the declaration could not be saved
  Then I see an error and can try again
  And I do not reach the wardrobe
```
