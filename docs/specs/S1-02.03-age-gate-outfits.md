## User story

```gherkin
Feature: Red acceptance: outfits are behind the 18+ confirmation
  As a considered dresser
  I want the age gate for outfits behaviour checked by acceptance tests
  So that the team knows it works before any code is written
```

Context: [S1-02](S1-02-manual-outfit-creation.md) (manual outfits from wardrobe photos, device-only). Scope is frontend-only unless the brief says otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: A person who has not confirmed their age cannot reach outfits

  When I try to open my outfits
  Then I am asked whether I am 18 or over
  And I cannot create an outfit
```
