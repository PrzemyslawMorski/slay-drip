## User story

```gherkin
Feature: Outfits store persists across reloads
  As a considered dresser
  I want my saved outfits to stay correct on this device
  So that outfits never point at photos I have removed or withdrawn
```

Context: [S1-02](S1-02-manual-outfit-creation.md) (manual outfits from wardrobe photos, device-only). Scope is frontend-only unless the brief says otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Saved outfits are still there after the app is reloaded
  Given I have saved an outfit named "Weekend" with 2 wardrobe items
  When I reload the app
  Then I see "Weekend" in my outfits
  And "Weekend" lists its 2 items
```
