## User story

Requirement served: F2.2 single-item capture and the wardrobe view that follows it, with no account (spec [S1-01](S1-01-anonymous-wardrobe-upload.md)).

```gherkin
Feature: Consent prompt before the first photo; declining keeps the wardrobe empty
  As a considered dresser
  I want the anonymous wardrobe behaviour in S1-01
  So that this ticket's scenarios can be checked against the spec
```

Frontend only. Photos stay on the device under [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-on-device-wardrobe-storage.md).

## Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Consent to store photos is asked before the first photo is added
  Given I have confirmed that I am 18 or over
  When I choose "Add photos"
  Then I am asked whether to store photos of my clothes on this device
  And no photo is stored yet

Scenario: Declining consent keeps the wardrobe empty
  Given I have confirmed that I am 18 or over
  And I have been asked whether to store photos of my clothes on this device
  When I decline to store photos
  Then I see my wardrobe
  And my wardrobe is empty
```
